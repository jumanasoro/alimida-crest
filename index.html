Welcome to Alimida Crest

A relaxed, green hideout just off the highway

Alimida Crest sits along the highway in Lungalunga, just before Perani. It is not a fancy place, and that is the point: honest prices, clean rooms, friendly faces and a quiet walled compound full of mango, palm and flowering shrubs. Stop for a night on the road, come for a meal and a drink, or slip away for a quiet weekend.

  • Right on the highway in Lungalunga, easy to find and easy to leave early
  • Secure walled compound with gated parking for cars and lorries
  • Garden gazebos for a quiet meal, a drink or a phone call
  • Friendly, down-to-earth service at prices that make sense

Bar & Restaurant

Good food, cold drinks and the big match

Our bar and restaurant is where Lungalunga comes to unwind. Grab a hot meal and a cold drink, sit out in one of the garden gazebos, or join the crowd to watch the football. We stock your favourite Kenyan beers, ciders, wines, spirits and soft drinks.

  • Home-style meals, breakfast to dinner
  • Fully stocked bar with cold beer and soft drinks
  • Private gazebos set in a landscaped garden
  • Live football and big sports matches on screen
  • Open to guests, locals and passing travellers

Match Day

Come watch the football with us

Premier League weekends, Harambee Stars games, Champions League nights: if the big match is on, it is on at Alimida Crest. Pull up a chair, order a cold one and something from the kitchen, and enjoy the game with the neighbourhood.

  • Big matches and sports shown live
  • Cold drinks and food served through the game
  • Everyone welcome, guests and locals alike

Conference Hall

A conference hall set up the way you need it

Our hall has a high timber roof, bright windows on both sides, a raised stage and tiled floors. Tell us what your event is and we will arrange the tables and chairs for you before you arrive, then keep the tea, meals and drinks coming from our kitchen and bar.

  • Raised stage for the speaker, head table or screen
  • Tables and chairs set in your choice of layout
  • Power points along the walls, good natural light
  • Tea breaks, lunch and drinks served on site
  • Beds on site for guests who travel in
